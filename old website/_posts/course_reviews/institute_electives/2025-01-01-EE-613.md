# Autumn 2023
### Prof. Debasattam Pal
**Author**: Vighnesh JR

**Pre-requisite courses**: Nil

**Pre-requisite skills**: Differential Equations, basic control theory, linear algebra

**Course Content**: 

- Nonlinear dynamical systems, an introduction and specialities.
- Linearisation and linear stability analysis.
- Poincare Bendixon theorem, Bendixon theorem, index of a field, bifurcations.
- Lyapunov stability, Asymptotic stability, Chetaev’s Instability theorem.

**Motivation to take up the course**: Interest in mathematics for controls or dynamical analysis.
