# Spring 2022
### Prof. Urban Larson
**Author**: Parth Nawkar

**Pre-requisite courses**: None (instructor consent required)



**Course Content in Brief**: 

- Divided into 2 parts: game theory and decision analysis. For our batch only game theory part was covered.
- Course involved a Project at the end.
- Course belongs to DS basket.
- Included fun games for first lecture.

**Motivation behind taking this course**: Interesting topic with various applications. Course from DS basket in case I want to complete this minor too. And if achieved good grade retag it as IE.
